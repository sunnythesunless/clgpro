## 🩺 2. How to Operate the Application

### Step 1: Account Creation & Login
1. Open [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173) (or `http://localhost:8000`).
2. Click **Create an account** or sign in with an existing account.
3. *(Test account created during verification: `radiologist_test@hospital.org` / `SecurePassword123!`)*.

### Step 2: Set Clinician Profile Credentials
1. Click **Profile** in the sidebar.
2. Enter your hospital name (e.g. *St. Jude Medical Center*), department (*Diagnostic Radiology*), and license number (*MD-94021*).
3. Click **Save Profile**. These credentials will appear on all digitally signed reports.

### Step 3: Upload Radiograph & Generate AI Report
1. Click **Generate Report** in the sidebar.
2. Select your modality: **Chest X-Ray**, **CT Scan**, **MRI**, or **Other**.
3. Choose a sample radiograph from `sample_images/` (e.g., `chest_xray_infiltrate.png`).
4. Click one of the **Intake Presets** (*Trauma Intake*, *Infiltrate / Pneumonia*, *Cranial CT*) to auto-populate patient demographics and clinical history.
5. Click **Generate AI Report**. The BLIP vision-language engine analyzes the scan and redirects to the detailed report.

### Step 4: Diagnostic PACS Viewer Controls
On the study detail page:
- **Tissue Window Presets**: Click `BONE`, `SOFT TISSUE`, `LUNG`, or `INVERT` to adjust contrast for specific tissue densities.
- **Explainable AI (XAI)**: Toggle **AI Attention Heatmap** to reveal color-coded activation zones where the AI detected abnormalities. Click any zone to lock crosshairs onto the region of interest.
- **Calibration Reticle**: Click the grid icon to overlay anatomical scale grids.

### Step 5: Prior Study Comparison (Longitudinal Tracking)
1. In the **Compare With Prior Study** dropdown, pick any earlier study for this patient.
2. Switch between 3 comparison modes:
   - **Dual PACS**: Synchronized side-by-side view with locked zoom and windowing.
   - **Curtain Swipe**: Interactive slider to inspect structural differences.
   - **Blink Mode**: Rapidly alternates images to highlight newly developed lesions.
3. Read the **AI Interval Summary** for automated progress notes.

### Step 6: AI Radiologist Copilot & Patient Translation
- **AI Copilot Tab**: Ask clinical questions regarding ACR Appropriateness Criteria®, Fleischner Society guidelines, or differential diagnoses.
- **Patient Summary Tab**: Converts clinical findings into a plain-English 6th-grade summary with key takeaways and questions to ask the doctor.
- **Medical Glossary**: Click **Medical Glossary** in the sidebar for searchable terminology and pronunciations.

### Step 7: Sign-Off & Multi-Format Export
- Click **Edit / Sign-off** to amend findings, enter internal notes, and click **Sign & Finalize Report** to apply a digital stamp.
- Click **Export .TXT** to download a plain-text clinical report.
- Click **Print / PDF** to print a formal hospital-headed report.
- From **Report History**, click **Export All (CSV)** to download a complete research archive of all cases.

## 🐳 3. How to Deploy

### Option 1: Docker / Docker Compose (Recommended)

Run the fullstack system in a production container with persistent volumes:

```bash
# Build and run in detached mode
docker compose up --build -d

# Check running status
docker compose ps

# View live container logs
docker compose logs -f
```
- Access the application at **`http://localhost:8000`**.
- Database records and scans are saved in the `medreporter_data` Docker volume.

To stop:
```bash
docker compose down
```

---

### Option 2: Cloud Deployment on Render (1-Click Blueprint)
1. Push your repository to GitHub.
2. In [Render](https://render.com), choose **New Blueprint Instance** and connect the repository.
3. Render automatically detects [render.yaml](file:///d:/clgpro/render.yaml), sets up the Docker build, and mounts a persistent 10GB disk for uploaded scans and SQLite database.

---

### Option 3: PaaS (Railway / Heroku)
The project includes a [Procfile](file:///d:/clgpro/Procfile). When deploying to Railway or Heroku:
1. Connect the repository.
2. Add the following environment variables in your dashboard:
   - `ALLOWED_ORIGINS=["*"]`
   - `SECRET_KEY=<generate-a-random-secret-key>`
   - `DEVICE=cpu`
   - `DATABASE_URL=sqlite+aiosqlite:///./data/medical_reports.db`
